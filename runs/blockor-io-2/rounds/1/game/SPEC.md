# blockor.io: what the footage shows

## How it played

- **core loop**: Spawn as a single 1x1 block, roam the arena collecting stray 1x1 blocks so they snap onto your grid and merge into larger glossy square gems, use A/D to rotate and W to shed/shoot blocks for speed or symmetry, avoid white obstacle blocks that shatter your structure, and ram smaller players' core/heart blocks to shatter and absorb their mass while climbing to #1 on the leaderboard.
- **why players loved it**: Players loved the satisfying spatial puzzle of aligning 1x1 blocks into massive, gleaming gem squares (or 'perfect pearls'), the custom YouTuber skins that tiled across every square in their cluster, the high-stakes risk/reward of spinning and squeezing between deadly white obstacle blocks, and the ability to outmaneuver bigger players by shedding mass to stay fast or exposing/protecting a specific core/heart block.
- **moment to moment**: Fast and nimble when small, transitioning into a heavy, deliberate juggernaut as your central square grows. Combat is tense and positional—players constantly rotate their clusters to bring their largest merged blocks to bear against an opponent's vulnerable core or heart block while watching their rotation energy bar and dodging mines.
- **together**: Players competed in real-time Free For All and Team arenas across PC and mobile, hunted down the #1 leaderboard holder in packs, used Party links to join friends' servers, and frequently teamed up or fed blocks to favorite streamers by shooting blocks with W or intentionally shattering themselves on white obstacles.

## Rules

Each rule cites the footage (video key and mm:ss). Rule numbers are stable; testers cite them.

### Player Character & Block Merging

1. A player spawns as a single 1x1 square gem block with their nickname displayed across their main/core block. (A 00:35, C 00:49, G 00:01)
2. Touching small stray 1x1 blocks floating on the map attaches them to the player's block structure at the point of contact on a grid alignment. (A 00:42, C 00:55, G 00:18)
3. When attached blocks form a complete solid square (e.g., 2x2, 3x3, 4x4, up to giant squares), they automatically merge into a single larger glossy gem block. (A 00:47, B 00:41, G 01:00, G 10:00, H 00:46)
4. Players can build complex multi-block structures with multiple merged squares of varying sizes or hollow ring shapes attached to their core block. (B 06:28, H 04:13)
5. In the later update (Feb 2017 / mobile release), the player's core block is represented by a specific 2x2-sized Heart Block marked with a heart icon, rather than the largest merged block. (D 01:21, D 02:31, D 09:05)

### Controls & Movement

6. The player's block cluster moves continuously toward the mouse cursor position on PC (or virtual directional arrow on mobile). (C 00:55, D 00:26, E 00:18)
7. Pressing A rotates the player's block cluster counter-clockwise and pressing D rotates it clockwise. (A 00:51, B 12:45, E 00:18, G 11:44)
8. Rotating with A or D consumes energy from the vertical green bar on the bottom-left and grants a temporary movement/rotational speed boost. (A 00:55, B 09:06, D 03:33, H 07:19)
9. In the later update (Video D), rotating/spinning causes unmerged 1x1 outer blocks to detach and fall off the player's structure. (D 03:22, D 03:40)
10. Pressing W shoots/ejects a 1x1 block from the player's structure toward the cursor, which also consumes a small amount of the green energy bar. (A 01:07, B 12:44, B 17:41, E 00:18)
11. In the initial version (Videos A, B, C, E, F, H), shooting blocks with W can break down the player's own main block into smaller blocks to regain high movement speed. (B 02:33, B 08:16, B 10:03)
12. Movement speed decreases as the player's main block / total mass grows larger. (A 14:50, B 02:17, B 12:24, H 03:17)

### Camera

13. The camera is top-down 2D, centered on the player's core block, and dynamically zooms out as the player's block structure grows larger. (A 00:35, A 03:38, B 13:12)

### Combat, Obstacles & Win/Lose Conditions

14. When two players collide, larger blocks destroy smaller blocks on contact, shattering them into collectible 1x1 blocks. (A 03:17, B 01:44, H 01:59)
15. In the initial version, a player dies immediately if their main (named) block is destroyed by a larger enemy block; losing outer/secondary blocks does not kill the player. (A 07:31, B 06:52, H 03:36)
16. In the later Heart Block update (Video D), a player dies if and only if their Heart Block is destroyed. (D 02:49, D 09:19, D 13:53)
17. White/grey square obstacle blocks are scattered throughout the arena and form a continuous perimeter wall around the map. (A 00:46, B 05:25, C 04:07, D 01:20)
18. If a player's outer/secondary block touches a white obstacle block, that block explodes/shatters into small 1x1 blocks; if the player's main/core block touches a white obstacle block in the early version, the player dies or their main block shatters. (A 01:42, A 02:15, B 06:08, B 07:15, E 06:53, G 06:12)
19. If a white obstacle block is hit enough times, it is destroyed and disappears from the map. (E 06:03, F 05:05)
20. In the later update (Video D), large neutral colored gem blocks ('food blocks') spawn around the map; players with sufficiently large blocks can ram them to shatter them into a cluster of collectible 1x1 blocks. (D 01:28, D 02:53, D 03:11)

### Scoring, Decay & Progression

21. Each 1x1 block attached to the player's structure equals 1 point of score, which is tracked in real time on the leaderboard. (A 00:37, G 00:18, G 10:11)
22. In the later update (Video D), large players experience slow score decay (losing approximately 1 block per second) to prevent passive camping. (D 12:48, D 13:05)

### Menus, HUD & Customization

23. The HUD displays a 4-quadrant minimap in the top-left showing player dots, a vertical green energy/power bar (0 to 100) in the bottom-left, and a Top 10 leaderboard plus the player's current rank and score in the top-right. (A 00:37, D 01:20, G 00:01, H 05:23)
24. Players can select free cosmetic skins (solid colors or YouTuber/creator logos such as Kwebbelkop, MasterOv, Jelly, Slogoman, AzzyLand, FaZe Mojo) from the Skin menu, which apply to every merged square block in their structure. (E 00:04, F 02:18, F 08:16, F 11:17)
25. The settings menu allows players to toggle a Dark Background theme and select a server region (Europe Frankfurt/London, N. America East/Central/West/South, Asia Tokyo/Singapore). (F 10:52, G 00:01)

### Multiplayer & Social

26. The game supports cross-platform multiplayer between web browsers (PC) and mobile devices (iOS/Android), as well as Party codes (Create/Join) and Free For All / Teams modes. (A 00:01, D 00:14, D 07:17)

### Audio

27. In the later update (Video D), popping/collecting sound effects play when picking up small blocks or shattering large blocks. (D 00:27, D 01:55, D 10:20)
