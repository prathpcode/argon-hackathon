# Glitch: what the footage shows

## How it played

- **core loop**: Players explore interconnected 2D streets to harvest quirky resources (mining rocks, watering bubble/bean trees, squeezing chickens, massaging butterflies), manage their Mood and Energy meters via food, drinks, and social buffs, complete whimsical quests for Giant favor and currants, train real-time skills across a 133-skill web, and customize their avatars and personal Home Streets.
- **why players loved it**: Players loved its completely original, surreal humor andunderground-art aesthetic, its non-violent cooperative sandbox nature, the seamless integration between the browser web portal (profiles, snaps, wardrobe, skill trees) and the live Flash world, and the countless charming details—from gender-neutral avatar customization and wind-up Cubimal races to melancholic musicblocks and lore about the 11 Giants.
- **moment to moment**: The pace is relaxed, whimsical, and unhurried. Movement is smooth with a floaty, low-gravity jump that makes hopping onto ledges, trees, and rooftops feel weightless and playful. Point-and-click interactions pop up responsive verb menus accompanied by cheerful chimes, quirky animations, and atmospheric, biome-specific acoustic and ambient soundtracks.
- **together**: Players gathered on streets and Home Streets to mine rocks cooperatively for bonus yields, form follow-train conga lines, high-five, hug, match daily 'hi' signs, shower each other with Random Kindness rainbows and Sparkle Powder, drink Pumpkin Ale to trigger synchronized Thriller dances, race Cubimals, listen to Musicblocks together, and contribute to server-wide Feats.

## Rules

Each rule cites the footage (video key and mm:ss). Rule numbers are stable; testers cite them.

### player character

1. The player avatar ('Glitchen') is a 2D side-view cartoon character with customizable gender-neutral proportions, facial features, hair, clothing layers, masks, and rideable costume mounts. (A 05:02, A 07:16-08:00, A 11:44-13:05, B 00:06)
2. Avatar movement consists of horizontal walking left/right and a low-gravity, floaty jump arc that allows landing on elevated terrain ledges, tree branches, and multi-tiered house roofs. (A 02:47-02:53, A 08:03, A 09:17-09:24, B 06:08-06:15)
3. Avatars have distinct animations for idle breathing/blinking, walking, jumping, mining with a pickaxe, watering/tending plants, drinking/tasting, hugging, high-fiving, squeezing chickens, and synchronized dancing. (A 03:48, A 08:26, A 24:31, A 29:43, A 53:11, B 04:24, B 05:59)
4. Temporary buffs/consumables can shrink avatars to miniature size or enlarge ('embiggen') them to giant size while retaining full movement. (A 43:35-43:48, A 57:31-57:38)

### controls

5. Clicking any interactive world object, NPC, other player, or inventory item opens a context-sensitive popup menu of verbs (e.g., 'Talk to', 'Mine', 'Pet', 'Squeeze', 'Drink', 'Taste', 'Give', 'Drop', 'Bestow', 'Play'). (A 08:25, A 19:16, A 23:25, A 50:30, B 03:51, B 03:56, B 05:50)
6. Pressing number keys triggers social emotes (such as key '5' to wave 'hi', or 'bye' during the final shutdown hour), and pressing 'Tab' cycles between active chat channels. (A 19:37-19:48, A 27:41)
7. Horizontal movement and jumping are controlled via keyboard directional keys and jump key independently of the mouse cursor. (A 02:47, A 09:18, B 06:08; inferred)

### camera

8. A 2D side-scrolling camera tracks the player horizontally with multi-layer parallax backgrounds and foreground silhouettes, clamping at the left and right edges of each street or interior room. (A 01:40-02:04, B 00:10-00:20, B 03:38-03:50, B 05:53-06:28)

### levels and areas

9. The game world ('Ur') is divided into regions (e.g., Zhambu, Kalavana, Andra, Groddle, Uralia), each composed of interconnected 2D side-scrolling streets linked by signposts at street boundaries and doors to interior rooms. (A 01:46, A 10:28-10:45, B 00:11, B 03:30-03:38, B 05:39-05:45)
10. Walking into a signpost path or clicking a building door triggers a loading screen ('Entering [Street] in [Region]', 'recalculating splines...') that transitions the player into the destination area. (A 24:36-24:39, B 03:34-03:37, B 05:30-05:36, B 05:41-05:44)
11. Every player has a persistent, publicly visitable 'Home Street' featuring an exterior house, resource nodes, project slots, and a Magic Rock that allows changing the street's visual and musical theme (e.g., 'Lost Woods', 'Vam Road') for 190 energy. (A 11:03-11:15, A 31:52-32:45)
12. Players can teleport directly to streets via the World Map, return to their Home Street via the top-left menu ('Go home'), or accept summon requests from other players. (A 10:41, A 24:48-24:51, A 26:28)

### npcs and creatures

13. There is no combat or enemies; all interactions with creatures and NPCs are social, economic, or harvesting-based. (A 01:30-03:00, B 05:44-06:05)
14. The 11 Giants (including Cosma, Zille, Humbaba, Alph, Lem, Grendaline) are the gods of Ur; players earn Favor with them via quests and street shrines, and during special events their giant animated masks appear on-screen with messages. (A 03:52-04:07, A 24:43, A 44:31-44:49, A 48:02-48:24, A 54:57-55:08, B 00:53, B 02:57-03:15)
15. A Butler NPC stands outside the player's house on their Home Street, automatically greeting/waving to visitors and offering a menu to check visitor logs ('Who visited?'), manage gifts/mail, and change poses. (A 01:19, A 19:16-19:23, A 51:16)
16. Street Spirits act as themed item vendors on streets (e.g., Animal Goods Vendor, Kitchen Tools Vendor), opening a Buy/Sell interface priced in Currants. (A 24:37, B 05:48-05:52)
17. Bureaucrat NPCs inside the Andra Bureaucratic Hall require players to stand still in a designated Waiting Area for a 10-second countdown timer before speaking to a Magister to buy qualifications (e.g., Card-Carrying Qualification for 50 currants). (B 04:30-05:25)
18. Wild animals roam streets—such as Chickens (which can be clicked to 'Squeeze' for +1 mood), Butterflies (massaged with lotion or milked for quests), and Piggies (petted or hogtied for quests). (B 02:59, B 03:10-03:15, B 05:45, B 05:58-06:02)
19. Cubimals are wind-up miniature creatures in the player's inventory that can be released on a street to race a random distance measured in 'planks' before stopping. (A 23:21, A 23:35, A 23:44, A 24:16-24:20)

### harvesting and nodes

20. Resource nodes on streets include Beryl Rocks (mined with a pickaxe for -7 Energy, yielding +10 iMG and Chunks of Beryl, with bonus chunks when mined cooperatively), Wood/Fruit/Bubble Trees (watered, tended, or petted), Dirt Piles (dug with a shovel), and construction projects (Tower). (A 02:33, A 03:48, A 08:24-08:36, B 02:58-03:08, B 06:21)

### items and inventory

21. The bottom HUD bar holds 16 main inventory slots (which can hold container bags for sub-slots), a 'Furniture' tab toggle, and a text search bar. (A 00:05, A 10:18, B 00:06, B 03:15)
22. Social/party items trigger shared audio-visual effects: 'Random Kindness' arcs a rainbow over a target player (+18 to +40 mood), 'Sparkle Powder' fills the screen with stars (+10 mood), 'Pumpkin Ale' gives +29 mood and triggers a group Thriller dance, and 'Musicblocks' play music tracks for -2 Energy (+30 iMG, +2 mood). (A 03:21, A 09:54-10:02, A 23:25-23:40, A 29:41-29:45, A 33:54-34:01, A 50:30-50:45, A 54:13-54:20)
23. Consumables and utility items have specific verb actions: 'Butterfly Lotion' can be tasted (+12 mood, consuming it), 'Water Cooler' dispenses a 'Cup of Water' (-2 energy) which can be drunk (+5 mood), and a '12-sided Die' can be rolled to broadcast random outcomes in chat. (A 20:01-20:04, B 03:51-04:26)

### scoring and currencies

24. The game tracks four main currencies/points: iMG/XP (earned from actions, quests, and visitors; used to level up), Currants (in-game money for vendors/auctions), Credits (premium/subscription currency for Wardrobe cosmetics), and Giant Favor (earned via quests/shrines). (A 00:05, A 04:45, A 07:16, A 08:34, B 00:31-00:35, B 02:40, B 02:57-03:15)

### meters and timers

25. Two primary meters govern player activity: Mood (percentage/100 gauge at top-left that boosts iMG/XP multiplier and is raised by drinks, emotes, and social items) and Energy (numerical bar below portrait, e.g., 495 max at Lv19 or 100 max at Lv3, spent on mining/harvesting/street changes and restored by food/orbs). (A 00:12, A 05:05, A 08:34, A 32:00, B 00:31-00:42, B 06:16-06:26)
26. Floating orbs on streets can be collected by jumping into them to gain +1 XP, +1 currants, +1 mood, or +1 energy. (B 06:16-06:26)

### progression

27. There are 133 skills across 7 categories (Alchemy, Animals, Cooking, Gathering, Growing, Industrial, Intellectual) that train in real time (online or offline); learning more skills than one's Brain Capacity applies an escalating time penalty (up to 2764%) to training durations. (A 07:00-07:10, A 14:11-14:38, B 02:45-02:51)
28. Players progress by leveling up via iMG/XP, completing multi-step Quests from the Magic Rock, earning Badges/Achievements (e.g., Bush Whacker, Social Butterfly), and contributing to server-wide Feats. (A 05:07, A 15:58, A 41:17, A 50:51, A 51:55, B 02:53-03:20)

### win lose conditions

29. As a persistent social sandbox MMO, there is no win or lose state; attempting to refresh/leave displays a joke alert 'WAIT! You were just about to win the game!'. (A 30:30, A 37:52, B 05:45)

### menus and hud

30. The Flash client HUD consists of a top-left avatar portrait (Mood, Energy, iMG, navigation menu), a top bar (Cultivate, Street name, World Map, audio sliders, fullscreen, settings), a bottom 16-slot inventory bar, and a right-hand sidebar (in-game calendar/clock, chat tabs, 'People here' list, and Local Chat/Activity log). (A 00:05, A 04:45, A 10:27, A 27:19, A 29:58, B 00:06)
31. Character management features (Profile, Wardrobe, Vanity, Skills, Friends, Snaps, Auctions, Forums) open in integrated web browser tabs that update the live Flash game session when saved. (A 05:00-07:55, A 11:24-13:12, A 21:50-22:01, A 23:41)

### other players

32. Players on the same street see each other in real time, can chat via overhead speech bubbles and the right-hand chat log, and can click each other to High five (+30 mood), Hug (+2 mood), Follow (forming automatic movement chains), Summon, Trade, or Give items. (A 08:25-08:36, A 24:31, A 26:15-27:12, A 28:47-29:35, A 33:49, A 53:11)
33. Waving 'hi' (or 'bye') to another player who has the same daily 'hi sign' (e.g., 'Bats') awards a matching bonus (+5 mood, stacking to 2x +10 mood). (A 19:37-19:43)

### audio

34. Each street style and region has its own looping background music (e.g., eerie plucked strings in Lost Woods, upbeat quirky rhythm in Vam Road, acoustic guitar in Andra), which can be overridden by playing Musicblocks or drinking Pumpkin Ale. (A 00:01-01:00, A 29:43, A 32:05-32:32, A 50:40-51:38, B 00:06-02:00)
