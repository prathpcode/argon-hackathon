## Rules

Each rule cites the footage (video key and mm:ss). Rule numbers are stable; testers cite them.

### player avatar

1. Avatars are 2D side-scrolling cartoon characters with large heads, customizable facial features (eyes, nose, mouth, ears, hair), skin/hair colors, and layered clothing (hats/masks, shirts/tops, coats/cloaks/capes, pants/shorts, skirts, dresses, shoes) without fixed gender selection. (A 07:16, A 11:44, A 12:53, B 00:06)
2. Avatars have distinct animations for idle breathing/blinking, walking left/right, jumping (arms/hair lifting, legs tucked), falling, climbing ladders, watering, mining with a pickaxe, and synchronized group zombie-dancing (Thriller). (A 01:40, A 02:47, A 03:48, A 08:26, A 28:20, A 29:43)
3. Players can jump onto elevated terrain ledges, tree/rock platforms, and multi-tiered house roofs; jumping is arc-based with mid-air horizontal control. (A 08:03, A 09:18, B 06:10, B 06:20)
4. Consumables or buffs can temporarily alter avatar scale, shrinking players to miniature size or embiggening them to giant size while preserving normal movement and animations. (A 43:35, A 43:44, A 57:08, A 57:36)

### camera and parallax

5. The camera tracks the player horizontally (and vertically on tall streets) within bounded 2D street scenes, with multi-layered parallax scrolling backgrounds (foreground ground plane, mid-ground trees/hills, distant sky/mountains). (A 01:40, A 24:40, B 00:11, B 06:04)
6. A mini-map strip in the top-right of the game viewport shows the current street's layout, player position dot, other players' dots, and a 'World' button to open the full World/Region Map. (A 00:10, A 10:27, B 00:06, B 01:06)

### streets and navigation

7. The game world is divided into regions (e.g., Zhambu, Kalavana, Andra, Ixeta) composed of interconnected streets shown as dashed lines and nodes on the Region Map. (A 10:28, A 10:33, A 24:36, B 03:34)
8. Streets connect at their left/right boundaries via wooden signposts that display adjacent street names (or neighbor home streets) and trigger a loading screen ('Entering [Street Name] in [Region]', 'reticulating splines...') when walked past. (A 01:46, B 00:11, B 05:38, B 05:41)
9. Buildings on streets (such as the player's house or the Ministry of Departments) have interactive doors that highlight on hover and transition the player into an interior street/room when clicked or entered. (A 11:08, B 02:29, B 03:33, B 05:29)
10. Visiting a street for the first time awards a small XP bonus (e.g., +1 xp or +2 xp) and logs the visit in the local activity feed. (B 03:37, B 05:44)
11. Players can teleport directly to streets via the World Map ('Teleport here' using a teleportation token or energy) or return to their Home Street via the top-left menu ('Go home'). (A 10:41, A 24:48)

### creatures and npcs

12. Home Street Butler NPC (e.g., 'Roy (Beau)') stands outside the player's house, waves 'bye' to departing visitors, and offers a radial/popup menu with 'Who visited?', 'Muffle butterfly wisdom', 'Stop telling me things visitors say', 'Zip lip', 'Set pace', ' Yahoo', 'Gipe', 'Leave info', ' animations', and 'Mailbox'. (A 01:19, A 19:16, A 19:21)
13. Magic Rock on the player's Home Street can be clicked ('Talk to') to open a management menu: 'House keys', 'Customize', 'Change style' (allows buying new visual/audio street themes for 190 iMG), 'Expand', and 'Cultivate land'. (A 01:50, A 31:52, A 31:55, A 32:33)
14. Street Spirits (such as 'Animal Goods Vendor' or 'Kitchen Tools Vendor') are stationary NPCs on public streets that open a 'Buy / Sell' shop interface where players purchase tools and consumables with currants. (A 24:36, B 05:49, B 05:51)
15. Animals wandering on streets include Chickens (which can be squeezed for +1 mood), Piggies (which can be petted, hogtied, or fed crops to produce seed-bearing plops), and Butterflies (which fly in the air and can be massaged with Butterfly Lotion or milked with a Butterfly Milker). (A 30:38, B 02:59, B 03:10, B 05:51, B 05:56)
16. Cubimals (e.g., Chick Cubimal Lv1.1, Phantom Cubimal Lv1.1, Dustbunny Cubimal, Firebog Street Spirit Cubimal, Piggy Cubimal) are wind-up/ racing toy creatures that players set down from inventory to race along the ground, reporting distance traveled in 'planks' before stopping. (A 23:25, A 23:44, A 24:17, A 46:00)
17. Bureaucrat NPCs inside Andra Bureaucratic Hall (Director Wigglesbottom, Secretary Wigglesbottom, Magister Piksnikson) enforce waiting-room mechanics where the player must stand still in a designated 'WAITING AREA' for a 15-second timer before purchasing qualifications (such as Card-Carrying Qualification for 50c). (B 04:34, B 04:45, B 05:00, B 05:17)
18. The 11 Giants (e.g., Cosma, Zille, Humbaba, Alph, Lem, Grendaline, Mab, Ti, Spriggan) have dedicated shrines on streets where players can donate items for favor, and Giants can appear as full-screen floating visages with text announcements. (A 03:53, A 44:31, A 48:02, A 54:57, B 00:53, B 05:58)

### resources and harvesting

19. Trees/Plants seen on streets include Wood Trees, Fruit Trees (pink blossoms), Gas Plants (red/blue pods), Bean Trees, Bubble Trees, and Spice Plants; players interact with them to water, pet, or harvest resources. (A 01:43, A 01:51, A 03:48, A 09:46, B 02:58)
20. Mineral rocks (such as Beryl Rock) can be mined using a pickaxe ('Talk to' / 'Mine'); mining consumes Energy (-7 Energy) and yields iMG (+10 iMG) and ore chunks (+8 Chunks of Beryl), with bonus chunks awarded when multiple players mine the same rock simultaneously. (A 08:25, A 08:34)
21. Dirt Piles appear on streets and can be dug with a shovel (solo or cooperatively with another player for quests like 'It Takes Two' and 'Can You Dig It?') to obtain Lumps of Earth. (B 03:01, B 03:05, B 06:21)
22. Floating circular orbs called Quoins (including Qurazy quoins) hover above platforms and reward +xp, +mood, +currants, or +energy when a player jumps through them. (A 24:39, B 06:16, B 06:26)

### items and inventory

23. The bottom HUD bar holds 16 primary slots (which can hold individual items or bags/containers that expand into sub-slots along a top row when selected), plus a toggle between 'Inventory' and 'Furniture' modes and a text search filter. (A 00:10, A 10:18, A 23:54, B 00:06)
24. Clicking an item in the inventory opens a context menu with item-specific actions such as 'Eat', 'Drink', 'Taste', 'Use', 'Bestow', 'Play', 'Play for', 'Roll', 'Set out', 'Give', 'Drop', and 'Inspect'. (A 20:01, A 23:25, A 29:41, A 50:31, B 03:56)
25. Some items have stack limits per slot (e.g., Cup of Water 'Fits only one in a backpack slot', while planks/ores stack up to 16 or more), and attempting to acquire an item when all slots are occupied triggers a 'Your bag is full!' warning. (A 00:10, B 03:51, B 04:19)

### currencies

26. The game uses three main currencies/points: Currants (in-game coin currency used for vendors, qualifications, and auctions, shown at bottom-left in older UI or profile), iMG / Imagination (used for street upgrades, style changes, and leveling), and Credits (microtransaction/subscription currency used for wardrobe cosmetics). (A 05:03, A 07:16, A 31:58, B 00:06, B 05:51)

### meters and stats

27. The top-left HUD displays the player's Mood gauge (percentage circle or numeric ratio, e.g., 78% / 113/120), Energy bar (e.g., 430/495 or 98/100), and iMG / XP counter; eating food restores Energy, drinking beverages or receiving social actions restores Mood, and high Mood grants XP/iMG multipliers. (A 00:10, A 00:13, A 29:43, B 00:31, B 00:37)

### skills and learning

28. Skills are learned in real time (even while offline) one at a time; each skill has prerequisites (level, prior skills, quests, or upgrades) and a real-time countdown timer (ranging from hours to days), with a 'Brain Capacity' skill cap above which a percentage time penalty applies. (A 07:01, A 14:13, A 14:21, B 02:46)
29. Skills are organized into 7 categories: Alchemy, Animals, Cooking, Gathering, Growing, Industrial, and Intellectual, and learning time can be reduced by consuming special items like 'Emblem of Friendly'. (A 14:13, A 24:10)

### quests

30. The Quest Log tracks active quests with step-by-step objective counters, clickable item/location links, 'Tell me more' hints, and rewards consisting of currants, iMG/xp, energy, mood, recipes, and favor with specific Giants. (B 02:53, B 02:58, B 03:18)

### crafting and tools

31. Crafting and harvesting require specific tools in inventory (e.g., Knife & Board, Frying Pan, Blender, Watering Can, Shovel, Pickaxe, Butterfly Milker, Meat Collector) and combine ingredients according to learned skills/recipes. (A 03:48, A 08:26, A 50:52, B 02:57, B 03:03, B 05:51)
32.  streets can host multi-step construction projects (such as a 'Tower' project) where players contribute materials and labor to fill a completion percentage bar. (A 02:33, A 03:49)

### housing

33. Each player has a public Home Street (whichanyone can visit, harvest on, and hang out on) containing an exterior house, a Butler, a Magic Rock, and cultivatable land/trees, plus an instanced multi-room interior house that can be expanded and decorated with furniture. (A 01:40, A 11:03, A 17:32, A 31:52)
34. When other players visit a player's Home Street, the Butler records their names and the owner receives an iMG reward the following day based on visitor traffic. (A 19:05, A 19:16, A 19:25; inferred)

### chat and social

35. The right-hand panel contains collapsible chat/activity panes: 'Local Chat / Activity' (showing local speech, system logs, arrivals/departures, and item/mood notifications), global channels ('Help', 'Global', 'Trade'), and tabbed private IM / party chats that can be cycled with the Tab key. (A 00:10, A 19:21, A 27:38, B 00:06)
36. Typing in Local Chat displays both a message in the right-hand chat log and a speech bubble above the player's avatar head in the street view. (A 09:33, A 25:34)
37. Clicking another player's avatar or name opens a social interaction menu: 'Open Profile Page', 'Block / Report', 'High five', 'Hug', 'Kick out [Player]', 'Imbue', 'Moon', 'Remove from Friends', 'Splank', 'Summon [Player]', 'Visit Home Street', 'Invite to Party', 'Offer to Trade', 'Give Something', and 'Follow'. (A 33:49, A 40:58)
38. Players can form a conga line by using 'Follow' on a leader (requiring the leader to click 'OK' on a confirmation prompt), causing followers' avatars to automatically walk and jump along the leader's path. (A 26:15, A 27:08, A 28:45)
39. Players can greet each other with daily 'hi' signs (e.g., 'bye', 'o/'); if two players have the same sign of the day, both receive a '2x / +10 mood!' match bonus. (A 19:37, A 19:41)

### menus and hud

40. Clicking the player avatar portrait in the top-left opens a vertical navigation menu ('Profile', 'Skills', 'Vanity', 'Wardrobe', 'Groups', 'Home', 'Keys', 'Encyclopedia', 'Auctions', 'Preferences', 'Help', 'Exit the World'); most of these links open in a companion browser tab on glitch.com that syncs live with the Flash game client. (A 04:45, A 05:00, A 23:41)

### sounds and music

41. Each street style has its own ambient background music track, and interactive items/events trigger distinct audio (e.g., Giant fanfare chimes, Thriller funk music when drinking Pumpkin Ale, and vocal/instrumental tunes when playing Musicblock items like GNG or DG-2); volume sliders for Effects and Music are accessible via the speaker icon in the top-right. (A 03:52, A 29:43, A 32:06, A 35:27, A 50:39)
