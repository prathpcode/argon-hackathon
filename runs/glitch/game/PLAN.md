# Whim — Revival Plan

## Core Loop
Players explore a whimsical, interconnected 2D side-scrolling world of vibrant regions and personal Home Streets inside the collective imagination of the Eleven Giants. Moving with smooth, forgiving platforming controls, players leap across ledges, roofs, and rocks to grab floating Quoins (XP/iMG, Mood, Energy, Currants), harvest quirky trees and mineral rocks (Beryl Rock, Fruit Trees, Wood Trees, Gas Plants, Dirt Piles), interact with eccentric creatures (squeezing Chickens, petting Piggies, milking/massaging Butterflies, racing wind-up Cubimals), donate at Giant Shrines for favor and full-screen Giant blessings, endure bureaucratic waiting rooms to earn qualifications, learn real-time skills, craft food and drinks to replenish Energy and Mood, customize their big-headed cartoon avatar, and team up with other players on the same street for co-op mining/digging, synchronized Thriller zombie dances, conga lines, and building community Tower projects.

## What Made the Original Fun
- **Immediate, Tactile Whimsy**: Every tree, rock, animal, and shrine glows cyan on hover and pops up a snappy, humorous speech-bubble interaction menu ("Squeeze", "Pet", "Water", "Talk to", "Mine", "Dig", "Donate") that rewards you with bouncy animations, floating numbers, sound effects, and quirky items within seconds.
- **Interconnected World & Personal Space**: Walking past wooden signposts or stepping through doors seamlessly transitions between public Savanna/Cavern streets, bureaucratic halls, and each player's customizable Home Street (which can switch visual/audio themes via the Magic Rock and hosts a personal Butler who tracks visitors).
- **Rich Interlocking Systems**: Mood multiplies iMG/XP gains, Energy fuels harvesting and mining, Currants buy tools and qualifications from floating tiki Street Spirits and frog bureaucrats, and real-time Skills + Quests always give a clear next goal on screen.
- **Playful Multiplayer Socializing**: Seeing other big-headed avatars hop around, chatting with overhead speech bubbles, matching daily "hi" signs (`o/`) for 2x Mood bonuses, forming automatic conga lines ("Follow"), getting bonus ore chunks when mining together, and triggering street-wide Thriller zombie dances.

## Ordered Feature List

### 1. First Minute Experience & Core Feel
1. **Instant Entry & Responsive Platforming**: Single keypress or click from the title overlay drops the player straight onto **Chego Chase in Andra** (or their Home Street), with smooth horizontal acceleration, friction, arc-based jumping with coyote time and jump buffering, climbable ledges/roofs, and smooth camera tracking with multi-layer parallax.
2. **Immediate Rewards in Path**: Floating Quoins (XP/iMG purple/white, Mood green, Energy yellow, Currants blue, and Qurazy quoins) hover within the first jump's reach; animals (Chickens, Piggies, Butterflies), harvestable trees/rocks/dirt piles, and a Shrine to Grendaline sit right along the street with glowing cyan hover outlines and pale-green pill tooltips.
3. **Full HUD Chrome & Audio**: Authentic `#EBF2F5` outer frame, top header with "Whim" logo, circular green Mood badge, green pill Energy bar, blue XP/iMG bar & Level counter, clickable googly-eyed Magic Rock quest/skill button, clock/date (`Primday, 23rd of Recurse`), sound/music volume toggles, top-right mini-map strip with player dots and World Map button, bottom-left spinning gold Currants counter, 16-slot bottom inventory bar with expandable bag sub-slots and Inventory/Furniture filter, and right-hand Local Chat / Activity / Global / Party sidebar.

### 2. All Five Iconic Streets & Seamless Navigation
4. **Chego Chase in Andra**: Savanna grassland with acacia parallax, Shrine to Grendaline, Dirt Pile on a raised rocky ledge, ziggurat stone Ministry of Departments building with interactive double doors, wandering Piggy & Butterfly, floating Quoins, and wooden signposts to Bongul Beat (`left`) and Laru Lane (`right`).
5. **Andra Bureaucratic Hall**: Interior office accessed via the Ministry door on Chego Chase, featuring exit doors, reception desk, interactive Water Cooler (dispenses Cups of Water with 1-per-slot stack rule), 6 orange chairs under the `WAITING AREA` sign, frog-like bureaucrats (Director Wigglesbottom, Secretary Wigglesbottom, Magister Piksnikson), and the 15-second stationary "Please Wait" timer to unlock purchasing the Card-Carrying Qualification (50c).
6. **Bongul Beat in Andra**: West savanna street with wandering Chicken, floating tiki Animal Goods Vendor Street Spirit (Buy/Sell shop), Shrine to Humbaba, pink-blossomed Fruit Tree, raised rock platform, Dirt Pile, floating Quoins, and signposts.
7. **Goli Grabs in Kalavana**: Multi-tiered rocky cavern/cliff street with climbable ledges and bridges, Shrine to Lem, Kitchen Tools Vendor Street Spirit, Generic Green Bag on the ground, a Qurazy quoin with top banner notification, and signposts.
8. **Beau's Home Street**: Player-owned street defaulting to the dark spooky "Lost Woods" theme (and toggleable to snowy pine "Vorn Road" or sunny meadow via the Magic Rock for 190 iMG), containing the multi-story climbable crooked "House of Whimsy" (plus an interior room), Butler Roy (with full visitor log & radial menu), Magic Rock, two coral/cyan Gas Plants, Beryl Rock (mineable with pickaxe, turns to rubble, co-op bonus), pink Fruit Tree, three Wood Trees, Tower construction project (collaborative % completion bar), and signpost.
9. **World & Region Map + Street Loading Transitions**: Walking past signposts or clicking doors shows the authentic loading banner ("Entering [Street Name] in [Region]", "reticulating splines...") and awards a First Visit XP bonus (`+2 iMG`); clicking the World Map opens the interactive ocean/region map (Zhambu, Kalavana, Andra, Ixeta) with dashed street lines, clickable street nodes, and "Teleport Here".

### 3. Harvesting, Crafting, Inventory, Skills & Quests
10. **Harvesting & Tools**: Interacting with Wood Trees, Fruit Trees, Gas Plants, Beryl Rock (Pickaxe, -7 Energy, +10 iMG, +8 Chunks of Beryl), Dirt Piles (Shovel, solo or co-op digging for Lumps of Earth), Chickens (Squeeze for +Mood & Grain), Piggies (Pet/Feed for Plops), and Butterflies (Massage with Lotion / Milk with Milker).
11. **Inventory Context Menus & Consumables**: Clicking any inventory slot opens item-specific actions (`Eat`, `Drink`, `Taste`, `Use`, `Release`, `Combine/Cook`, `Bestow`, `Give`, `Drop`, `Inspect`); consumables include food/drinks, Pumpkin Ale (triggers Thriller zombie dance & funk track), Shrinking/Embiggening potions (scale avatar to mini or giant size), Musicblocks (GNG, DG-2), and wind-up Cubimals that race across the ground and report distance in planks.
12. **Quests, Skills & Giants**: Active Quest Log ("It Takes Two", "Can You Dig It?", "Administrative Patience", "Beryl Bounty", "Favor of the Giants") with step counters and rewards; real-time Skill learning across 7 categories (Alchemy, Animals, Cooking, Gathering, Growing, Industrial, Intellectual) with countdown timer and Emblem of Friendly accelerator; Giant Shrines that accept donations for Favor and trigger full-screen Giant visage announcements with fanfare.

### 4. Multiplayer, Social & Customization
13. **Live Multiplayer & Co-op**: Real-time position, animation, scale, outfit, and street sync via `Net`; overhead speech bubbles + Local/Global/Party chat; daily "hi" sign (`o/`, `hi`, `wave`) matching for `2x / +10 mood!` bonus; clicking other players for social menu (`High five`, `Hug`, `Follow` conga line, `Imbue`, `Splank`, `Give Something`, `Visit Home Street`); co-op mining/digging bonuses and shared Tower construction progress; full Avatar Wardrobe & Vanity modal in the top-left/top-right menu.
