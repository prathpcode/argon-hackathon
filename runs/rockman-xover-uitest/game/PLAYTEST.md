## Issues, most severe first

1. [medium, multiplayer] Skipping a 4-Player Master Boss Raid does not broadcast raidHit or update shared boss HP for room peers: In public/js/boss.js, applyDamageToBoss() syncs Master Raid boss HP across the room via game.net.set(bs.boss.sharedKey, bs.boss.hp) and game.net.send('raidHit', ...). However, skipBossBattle() (lines 259-288) decrements bs.boss.hp directly inside a while loop without calling applyDamageToBoss() or broadcasting the final HP/damage to game.net. If a player clicks the top-right SKIP button (or presses KeyS) during a 4-Player Master Boss Raid, their damage is applied locally only and never synced to other room players. (evidence: public/js/boss.js lines 190-193 (applyDamageToBoss sends raidHit and sets sharedKey) vs lines 259-288 (skipBossBattle mutates bs.boss.hp directly without net.set or net.send).)
2. [low, fidelity] Action Stage HP clamps at 1 HP instead of triggering a stage fail/penalty, and OVER-1 doesn't run off-screen on Stage Clear: In public/js/stage.js (damagePlayerInStage, line 174), st.hp is clamped to Math.max(1, st.hp - twoSegDmg) so the player can never be defeated in an Action Stage even after taking 14+ hits, and startBossBattle("stage") initializes bs.playerHp to full maxPlayerHp rather than carrying over or penalizing stage damage. Additionally, at Stage Clear (Video A 01:23-01:26), OVER-1 stops in place under the modal instead of sprinting off the right edge of the screen before the rewards modal opens. (evidence: Inspected public/js/stage.js line 174 (st.hp = Math.max(1, st.hp - twoSegDmg)) and lines 398-405 (Stage Clear transition); compare with Video A 01:23-01:26 where OVER-1 runs off the right edge on Stage Clear.)
3. [low, fidelity] Boss Battle Normal Shot and C.Shot share the same dash and single-projectile animation instead of a 3-pellet burst vs charged strike: In Video A (02:23-02:25, 02:31-02:33), a normal Shot in Boss Battle fires a 3-pellet burst of round yellow energy shots in quick succession, whereas a Charge Shot (C.Shot) performs the forward dash/large blast. In public/js/boss.js (choosePlayerAttack and renderBoss, lines 284-295, 488-493), both Normal Shot and C.Shot perform the same 260px forward dash and fire a single elliptical projectile. Differentiating Normal Shot (3-pellet buster burst from standing position) from C.Shot (dash-in + heavy charged blast) would make C.Shot attacks feel much more distinct. (evidence: Inspected public/js/boss.js lines 284-295 and 488-493; compare with Video A 02:23-02:33 (3-pellet normal shot burst) and A 10:21-10:45.)
4. [low, multiplayer] 4-Player Master Raid squad projectiles fire simultaneously rather than in a staggered sequential combo: In public/js/boss.js (choosePlayerAttack, lines 298-315), during a 4-Player Master Raid, all 3 squad allies spawn their projectiles at the exact same x-coordinate timing with identical velocity (vx: 1350) and targetX: 940, so their hits land almost simultaneously in a single frame cluster. Staggering each squadmate's lunge and shot by ~0.12s would make the 4-unit combo attack read clearly as a sequential team barrage. (evidence: Inspected public/js/boss.js lines 298-315 where raidSquad ally projectiles are pushed in a synchronous loop without staggered delay.)
5. [low, fun] Action Stage player HP clamps at 1 HP instead of triggering a stage defeat or recovery penalty at 0 HP: In public/js/stage.js (damagePlayerInStage, line 173), taking damage from enemies, enemyBullets, or ground hazards reduces st.hp by 2/28ths of maxHp, resets charge aura, and triggers the electric shock flinch, but clamps st.hp = Math.max(1, st.hp - twoSegDmg). Because HP is clamped at 1, taking 14+ hits in an action stage never triggers a stage fail, respawn penalty, or emergency E-Can recovery. (evidence: public/js/stage.js line 173: st.hp = Math.max(1, st.hp - twoSegDmg); keeps player HP at 1 even when all 28 segments are depleted.)
6. [low, fidelity] Master Boss Raid hub lacks cumulative Raid Damage (PT) ranking leaderboard and 9/9 Map Part unlock gate: In Video C 07:13-07:45 and Rule 27, the 4-Player Master Boss Raid screen displays a per-player damage ranking leaderboard (e.g., 1st place PT score across room/world participants), and per Rule 16 (Video A 06:06-06:12) collecting all 9 pieces of the 3x3 Map grid unlocks the area's Master Boss. In public/js/deck.js (lines 430-467), the Master Boss Raid can be launched before 9/9 Map Parts are collected and does not display a cumulative raid damage ranking table for room players. (evidence: Compare Footage C 07:13-07:45 (Master Boss damage ranking leaderboard with PT scores) and A 06:06-06:12 with public/js/deck.js lines 430-467.)
