# Rockman Xover: what the footage shows

## How it played

- **core loop**: Spend EP to run short (~30-second) auto-scrolling action stages 5 times per stage, shooting enemies to collect Zenny, Battle Memory cards, and Map Parts. At 100% stage progress, fight a turn-based Boss Battle to earn Armor Parts and unlock the next stage/world. Between runs, fuse duplicate cards to level up stats/skills, arrange color-matched 5-to-8 card decks on unlocked OVER armors, pull Gacha, and tackle Master Boss raids, Battle Arena PvP, or Battle Tower floors.
- **why players loved it**: Nostalgic fan service celebrating the 25th Anniversary of Mega Man: collecting hundreds of Battle Memory cards and armors representing every era of the franchise (Classic, X, Zero, ZX, Battle Network, Star Force, Legends) accompanied by energetic rock/chiptune arrangements of classic Mega Man themes.
- **moment to moment**: Fast, casual, one-handed mobile pacing. Action stages are brisk auto-runners focused on timing jumps and holding/releasing charge shots to pierce groups of 2-3 enemies or trigger screen-clearing Giga Crushes, while boss fights arehands-off turn-based stat checks where players watch skill cut-ins and time their Charge Shot.
- **together**: Asynchronous social RPG cooperation and competition: seeing 3 other players after every quest run, exchanging Friend Requests and daily bulk 'Cheers' for Zenny, inspecting friends' card decks/armors, teaming up in 4-player groups to chip down multi-million-HP Master Bosses, competing in Battle Arena rankings, and chatting in the built-in Lobi community boards.

## Areas

- **Crossover World / World 1 (Mega Man X1 Bay Area)** (A 00:18-00:37, A 00:44-01:26, A 05:06-05:11): A futuristic coastal metropolis and highway bridge stage featuring bosses from Mega Man X1: Chill Penguin, Spark Mandrill, Armored Armadillo, and Flame Mammoth.
- **World 2 (Mega Man X1 Night City)** (C 03:02-03:10, D 08:02-08:10): A nighttime cyberpunk city skyline featuring the remaining Mega Man X1 Mavericks: Storm Eagle, Flame Stag / Boomer Kuwanger, Launch Octopus, and Sting Chameleon, plus Dr. Doppler.
- **World 3 (Classic Mega Man 1 Industrial City)** (C 11:27-11:31, D 08:12-08:21): An orange-tinted industrial factory cityscape featuring Classic Mega Man 1 Robot Masters: CutMan, ElecMan, IceMan, FireMan, and Dr. Wily.
- **World 4 (Mega Man Battle Network Cyberworld)** (C 03:11-03:19, D 08:22-08:32): A digital cyberspace network interior featuring NetNavis from Mega Man Battle Network: AirMan.EXE, FireMan.EXE, GutsMan.EXE, NumberMan.EXE, and WoodMan.EXE.
- **Worlds 5 to 11 ( Additional Crossover Worlds)** (C 10:44-11:26): Additional themed worlds spanning Mega Man Star Force (World 5), Mega Man Zero (World 6), Classic/X/Legends/ZX worlds (Worlds 7-11) with distinct city, forest, sky, and fortress backgrounds.
- **World 12 & X Special World (Fortress Interior)** (C 03:20-05:25, C 05:51-06:28): A dark, metallic indoor fortress laboratory with blue canisters and yellow hazard stripes, featuring bosses like Vile (Vava) and X-series Mavericks.
- **Battle Arena & Battle Tower** (C 09:30-10:41, E 00:01-06:12): A virtual blue-grid arena for 1v1 PvP battles and a multi-branch tower facility where players climb floors fighting Rosso.M, Blu.D, Verde.R, Oro.S, Nero.L, and classic bosses.

## Rules

Each rule cites the footage (video key and mm:ss). Rule numbers are stable; testers cite them.

### Player Character & Movement

1. In Quest action stages, the player character (OVER-1) warps in from a vertical beam and automatically runs horizontally from left to right at a constant speed until reaching the end of the stage. (A 00:44-01:26, A 09:21-09:50, C 03:40-04:20)
2. The player character has a fixed horizontal screen position on the left third of the screen while the background and ground scroll leftwards. (A 00:44-01:23, A 05:27-05:57)

### Controls & Combat (Action Stages)

3. Action stages feature three on-screen virtual buttons on the right side of the screen: Jump (bottom-left button), Shot (bottom-right button), and Giga Crush (top-right button). (A 00:48-01:19, A 05:27-05:56)
4. Tapping the Jump button makes the player jump vertically to reach high-flying enemies, upper-tier platforms/turrets, or airborne item drops, and jump over ground hazards. (A 00:54-00:57, A 05:39-05:41, C 03:41-03:43)
5. When not firing, the player character automatically charges their buster, cycling through three aura colors: purple/blue (tier 1), yellow (tier 2), and red/pink (tier 3). (A 00:57-01:06, B 02:10-02:20)
6. Firing with a yellow aura pierces and defeats up to 2 enemies in a row; firing with a red aura pierces and defeats up to 3 enemies in a row; firing with a blue aura defeats 1 enemy. (A 01:01-01:06, A 05:30-05:32, A 08:21-08:23)
7. Defeating multiple enemies simultaneously with a Charge Shot fills the blue bar under the Giga Crush button; when full, tapping Giga Crush leaps into the air and blasts 6 energy explosions across the screen, destroying all visible enemies. (A 01:11-01:22, A 14:41-14:45)
8. Later versions of the game include an 'AutoPlay' toggle button in the top-right corner of Quest action stages. (C 03:40-04:16, C 04:33-04:54)

### Camera

9. Both action stages and boss battles use a fixed 2D side-scrolling / side-view perspective with no vertical camera panning. (A 00:44-01:26, A 02:15-02:45)

### Quest Structure & Progression

10. Worlds are divided into 5 stages (4 regular boss stages + 1 locked final boss stage that unlocks after clearing the first 4), and each stage requires 5 auto-runner runs (20% progress per run) to reach 100% and unlock the stage's Boss Battle. (A 05:07-05:11, A 06:14-06:20, A 07:26-07:29, A 08:10-08:13, A 09:14-09:16, A 09:58-10:03, C 03:13-03:18)

### Boss Battles (Turn-Based)

11. Boss battles are turn-based ('Your Turn' alternating with 'Boss Turn') with a 50-turn limit ('残りターン数: 49' counting down each round) and a 'SKIP' button in the top-right corner to fast-forward to the battle outcome. (A 02:16-02:44, C 05:51-06:28, C 06:43-06:48)
12. During 'Your Turn', the player chooses between a normal Shot button and a Charge Shot (C.Shot) button; the C.Shot gauge starts at Lv. 0 and fills as the player deals and receives damage (up to Lv. 1 in early game or Lv. 5 in Battle Tower), unleashing a high-damage blast when used. (A 02:20-02:43, B 04:48-05:05, E 00:20-00:38)
13. Equipped Battle Memory cards can trigger passive/active skills at the start of a turn (e.g., ATK Up, DEF Up, enemy DEF Down, Heat DoT, Slow) before attacks are exchanged. (A 10:18-10:21, A 10:41-10:44, A 18:19-18:31, E 02:39-02:41)

### Win & Lose Conditions

14. In Boss Battles, whoever's HP bar reaches zero first loses. If the player loses, an 'Awakening Continue' (覚醒コンティニュー) screen offers a rematch with +20% HP/ATK/DEF and 2x skill activation rate for 100 Crest. (A 02:29-02:30, C 06:26-06:34, C 06:50-06:57)

### Drops, Map Parts & Armor Parts

15. Enemies defeated in action stages drop Zenny coins, green/gold Battle Memory cards, yellow EP recovery capsules, elemental chips, and Map Parts. (A 05:30-05:56, A 07:44-07:49, C 04:26-04:28)
16. Collecting all 9 pieces of a 3x3 Map grid in a World unlocks the Master Boss / Raid Boss for that area. (A 06:06-06:12, B 14:36-14:48, B 15:26-15:36)
17. Defeating a stage Boss for the first time awards one of 5 Armor Parts (head, body, right arm, left arm, legs); collecting all 5 parts unlocks a new playable Armor. (A 10:53-11:00, A 18:40-18:43, D 05:38-05:48)

### Armors & Battle Memory Deck

18. Each Armor (OVER-1 through OVER-10, OVER-B, OVER-Z, etc.) has its own elemental resistances, base stats, and a geometric grid of 5 to 8 Battle Memory card slots connected by lines. (A 02:04-02:11, C 01:50-01:57, C 02:10-02:42, C 08:53-08:59)
19. Each Battle Memory card has a Cost value, HP/ATK/DEF stats, a rarity (Normal, Normal+, Rare, SuperRare / SR, SR+, SSR), a skill, and a border color (red, green, blue, yellow, white/black). Total equipped card Cost cannot exceed the player's current max COST stat. (A 12:52-12:58, B 08:30-09:23, C 01:08-01:46)
20. Placing Battle Memory cards of matching colors into connected slots activates colored Bonus Lines (ボーナスライン) between them, significantly boosting total HP, ATK, and DEF, with the center card linking to all outer cards. (B 09:28-09:55, D 01:39-02:04, D 06:29-07:36)

### Card Enhancement (Upgrade)

21. In the Enhancement (強化) menu, players select a base Battle Memory card and fuse fodder cards into it at the cost of Zenny to increase its Level and stats; fusing cards with the same skill (e.g., Heat + Heat) also levels up the card's Skill Level. (C 05:26-05:39, D 03:48-05:09)

### Currencies & Gacha

22. The game uses two main currencies: Zenny (silver coins earned in stages, boss fights, login bonuses, and cheering friends) and Crest (gold coins, premium currency), plus Gacha Tickets and Arena Coins. (A 03:04-03:18, B 05:30-05:48, C 10:01-10:03)
23. Normal Gacha costs 200 Zenny per pull (or 1000 Zenny for a 5+1 bundle, or 20,000 Zenny for a 12-pull in later versions); Premium Gacha costs 300 Crest per pull. (A 03:09-03:23, C 09:11-09:15)

### Meters, Timers & Leveling

24.  Player progression is governed bythree top-bar meters: EXP (fills from stages to trigger Level Up), EP (Energy Points consumed by Quests and Bosses, regenerates 1 point every 5 minutes), and PP (5 pips used for Battle Arena PvP). (A 01:42-01:52, A 06:14-06:19, B 13:18-13:45, C 09:32-09:44)
25. Each Level Up grants 5 Status Points that the player manually distributes between EP (+1 max EP per 1 point) and COST (+1 max Cost per 2 points), and fully refills EP. (A 07:10-07:22, A 09:03-09:11, B 14:55-15:15)

### Multiplayer, Social & Modes

26. After each quest stage, 3 recently active players are displayed and can be inspected or sent Friend Requests; players can also search friends by an 8-character alphanumeric ID and use a bulk 'Cheer' (一括応援) button on their friends list to earn Zenny. (A 01:58-02:02, B 11:28-12:06, C 08:44-08:47, D 02:20-02:47)
27. Master Bosses (Raid Bosses) have millions of HP and are fought by a 4-player team of OVER-1 units simultaneously on screen, with a damage ranking leaderboard. (C 07:00-07:49)
28. Battle Arena is an asynchronous 1v1 PvP mode where players spend PP to fight other players' OVER-1 units for Arena Coins and ranking positions. (C 09:30-10:41)
29. Battle Tower (バトルタワー) is a floor-climbing boss gauntlet where boss HP persists across attempts (or recovers 1% over time) and clearing milestone floors (such as Floor 40) awards SR+ cards, E-Cans, H-Cans, and Zenny. (E 00:01-00:57, E 00:58-02:00, E 04:53-06:10)
