# Argon revival service (archive, lost game, revival workshop, play)

Mode: Operate. Visitors find a lost game, pick its footage, and watch Argon's agents bring it back: collect, remake, playtest to parity.

Audience/job: 13+ people who miss a shut-down game. Jobs: find the game, start a revival, follow the agents' work live, correct what's off, play it with friends.
Constraints: stats are real (dataset facts, live players, plays). The revival workshop is the product's centerpiece (user, 2026-10-25): it must show what every agent is doing in three stages.
